      Method and limits

      Places. OpenStreetMap: shop=second_hand, second_hand=yes/only, shop=charity (export of 95 records). Removed: . Same-name records within 50 m are counted once. Categories come from the OSM shop tag and the name.

      Buildings. 3D building models LoD2 Saxony (GeoSN): a place colours the building it sits in (or the nearest within 25 m). Trees: Baumkataster Stadt Leipzig, all in one colour, sized by crown diameter.

      Hinterland. Walking distance along OpenStreetMap paths and streets; 15 minutes = 1,200 m at 4.8 km/h. Residents: Zensus 2022, 100 m grid (Destatis).

      Limits. OSM may miss shops or hold closed ones; flea markets that are not permanent shops are not included. The age of customers or whether a shop is independent is not measured. A walk is not a visit.
